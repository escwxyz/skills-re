This page explains the cookies and similar storage we use on skills.re. We keep the set small and use it only for sign-in, remembering your last login method, language and theme preferences, consent, and optional analytics controls.

## Cookies We Set

- `session`: keeps you signed in while you browse the site. It expires at the end of your session.
- `locale`: remembers your language preference so the site stays in the language you chose. It lasts for 400 days.
- `theme`: remembers whether you prefer light or dark mode. It lasts for 400 days.
- `skills-re.last_used_login_method`: remembers the last sign-in method you used so we can highlight it next time. It lasts for 30 days.
- `cookie_consent`: stores your cookie preference so we do not ask you again on every visit. It lasts for 365 days.
- `cookiePreferences`: stores your functional and analytics preference for the site controls. It lasts for 90 days.

## Third-Party Cookies

We do not load third-party advertising scripts that set cookies. We only enable analytics cookies after you choose to allow them. If that changes in the future, we will update this page before introducing any new tracking cookies.

## Controlling Cookies

You can clear cookies through your browser settings at any time. Removing the `session` cookie will sign you out. Removing `locale`, `theme`, `skills-re.last_used_login_method`, `cookie_consent`, or `cookiePreferences` will reset those preferences to their defaults.

If you want to keep browsing while avoiding preference storage, you can still use the site, but some settings may reset between visits.
